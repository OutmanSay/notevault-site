Privacy Policy

NoteVault for Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM) · Effective Date: November 23, 2026
Plain-English Summary: NoteVault is 100% local-first. Your NotebookLM / Gemini Notebook chats never leave your browser. We have no servers, no analytics, and no tracking.

1. Data Stored Locally

NoteVault saves your Gemini Notebook / NotebookLM chat threads and extension settings exclusively in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local). This data stays on your device and is never uploaded to our servers or any third party.

2. Website Access

The extension only runs on https://notebooklm.google.com/* and https://notebook.google.com/* to detect chat messages on the active page and render the local export controls. It does not read or access any other websites or tabs.

3. Optional Pro License Verification

If you choose to purchase a one-time Pro license and enter your license key in the extension settings, NoteVault sends only the license key string to our payment processor (Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy) to verify whether the key is valid. No chat content, notebook titles, or browsing data is ever included in this request.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Analytics

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with any third parties. NoteVault contains zero analytics scripts, zero telemetry, and zero ads.

5. Deleting Your Data

You can delete individual saved threads inside the NoteVault History drawer or popup at any time, or remove all locally stored data by uninstalling the extension from Chrome.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us via the support link on the Chrome Web Store listing.
